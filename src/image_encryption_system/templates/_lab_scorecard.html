How to read this report

Every cipher is scored with the statistics image-encryption papers report, then attacked. The verdict comes from the attacks. Statistics are necessary, not sufficient: the hyperchaotic cipher below passes them and is still deterministic and unauthenticated, and the design family it follows was broken with chosen plaintexts (Rhouma & Belghith, 2008; see also Preishuber et al., 2018). Each statistical row is also a 5% test, so even a strong cipher misses one now and then by chance.
