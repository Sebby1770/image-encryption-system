What it does

  • Hides from a casual viewer that the picture carries a vault file.
  • Gives back exactly the same .ies file: ies uncloak, or ies decrypt on the PNG itself.
  • Changes each pixel by a few levels at most (about 45 dB PSNR at 2 bits).

What it does not do

  • It adds no secrecy: the encryption inside is what protects the image.
  • Steganalysis tools can still tell the picture has been cloaked.
  • Any recompression, resizing or editing destroys the hidden file. Share the PNG as a file, not through apps that recompress photos.
